# DurableMMO audio credits

Every audio file in this folder is listed below with its title, author, licence, source and changes. Licences were checked on 2026-11-01 against the licence text in each source repository (Kenney, Oculus pack, Beatscribe) or, for music originally published on OpenGameArt (which could not be reached from the build machine), against the per-track licence records in the repository the file was taken from, which state that each licence was verified on the track's own OpenGameArt page.

## Licences used

- **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication, no attribution required): all music, all Kenney sounds. Credited anyway.
- **CC BY 4.0** (attribution required): sounds from Oculus Audio Pack 01. Required credit line, which must ship with the game (e.g. in a Credits screen):

  > "Oculus Audio Pack 01" by Oculus VR, LLC (https://developers.meta.com/vr/downloads/package/oculus-audio-pack-1/), licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Sounds trimmed, pitched and re-encoded for DurableMMO.

- **CC BY 3.0** (attribution required): two spider sounds by columbia23 and one bat sound by suonho (Freesound), taken from VoxeLibre. Required credit lines:

  > "Spider" sounds by columbia23 (https://freesound.org/people/columbia23/sounds/395395/), CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), trimmed and re-encoded.
  > Bat sound by suonho (https://freesound.org/people/suonho/sounds/59344/), CC BY 3.0, re-encoded.

No CC-BY-SA, GPL, NC or ND material is included.

Music changes:

- title, home, village, riverside, night, festival: copied unchanged from Harvest Hollow, whose maintainers had already trimmed silence, added a 20 ms fade-in and a short fade-out (4-5 s for the two Memoraphile tracks), loudness-normalised linearly to -18 LUFS / -1 dBTP, and encoded Ogg Vorbis q0.6 and AAC-LC 64 kbit/s (.m4a, faststart). Their per-track records quote each OpenGameArt page's licence line (e.g. "License(s): CC0"; the two Memoraphile pages offer CC-BY 4.0 / OGA-BY 3.0 / CC0 and we use CC0).
- caves: loudness-normalised (linear loudnorm) from -12.5 LUFS to -18 LUFS, -1.5 dBTP, re-encoded Ogg Vorbis q0.6 and AAC 64 kbit/s. Length unchanged (60.0 s loop).
- battle: loudness-normalised to -18 LUFS, re-encoded Ogg Vorbis q0.6. It is a 4 s one-shot sting, not a loop.
