# LPC Art Pack — Credits

All art in this folder is Liberated Pixel Cup (LPC) style, collected from open repositories. Licenses: CC-BY-SA 3.0, GPL 3.0/2.0, OGA-BY 3.0, CC-BY 3.0, CC0 (many files are multi-licensed; you may pick any one of the listed licenses for that file). Where a file is CC-BY-SA, derivatives (including our composited character sheets and edited buildings) are shared under CC-BY-SA 3.0 as well. The LPC style guide and original assets: http://lpc.opengameart.org/ .

Sources used: https://github.com/liberatedpixelcup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator, https://github.com/ElizaWy/LPC, https://github.com/OpenGameArt/LiberatedPixelCup, https://github.com/DrJamgo/love2d-lpc-tiles (GPL-3.0), https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/RPG_Maker_MZ_LPC_Starter_Kit

Derived/composited files were produced by the DurableMMO project from the listed originals (layering, cropping, hue shift, adding banners/signs); they keep the originals' licenses and attribution.


## characters/

Each sheet was composited by layering PNGs from the Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator (https://github.com/liberatedpixelcup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator), recolored with the generator's own palette definitions. Layout: walk rows 0-3 (up, left, down, right; 9 frames of 64x64), slash rows 4-7 (6 frames). Per-layer credits below come from the generator's CREDITS.csv.

Every wilted frame is the matching stage drooped (squashed toward its base, outer leaves bent down) and recoloured toward dry brown; the dead frame is the small stage drooped further and recoloured brown-grey.
