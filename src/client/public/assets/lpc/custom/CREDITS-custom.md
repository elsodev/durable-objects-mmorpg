# Character Customization Layers — Credits

Every PNG in this folder is a layer cut from the Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator (https://github.com/liberatedpixelcup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator, commit 86160e7). There is one set of layers per body type, each built on that body's own generator base so the layers line up: **Male** = generator body `male` with head `head/heads/human/heads_human_male`; **Male (slim)** = generator body `teen` with head `head/heads/human/heads_human_male`; **Female** = generator body `female` with head `head/heads/human/heads_human_female`. Colors were produced with the generator's own palette definitions (palette_definitions/*: body, eye, hair, cloth; ulpc + lpcr palettes). Layout: 576x512 sheet of 64x64 frames; walk rows 0-3 (up, left, down, right; 9 frames), slash rows 4-7 (6 frames).

Folder layout: `<body>/<category>/<option>.png` for layers specific to one body, and `shared/<category>/<option>.png` for layers that are pixel-identical for several bodies (every hair and eye sheet, which the generator shares across adult bodies, and the slim legs and feet that Male (slim) and Female share). custom.json's `files` map points each body at its files. The "used by" column lists the bodies. Body ids: male = generator "male" base (regular build); male_slim = generator "teen" base (the slim build, flat chest) with the adult male head; female = generator "female" base (slim build) with the female head. The generator's female and teen bases have the same width; it has no slimmer adult base, and its "muscular" base has no shirts.

Actions: every layer also has an action sheet `<option>.act.png` (576x576: generator thrust rows 0-3 with 8 frames, sit rows 4-7 with 3 frames, hurt row 8 with 6 frames, from the same generator definitions). Tool layers live in `tools/<item>.bg.png` / `.fg.png`: the generator tool or weapon sprite split the generator's way into the part behind the body (zPos 8-9) and the part in front (zPos 150), re-laid out as 4 facing rows x the action's frames (custom.json `actions` / `tools`). The catalog characters' action sheets `../characters/<id>.act.png` are built from the same recipes and generator commit as their walk/slash sheets (`../characters/<id>.png`, credited in ../CREDITS.md); where the generator has no sit or thrust frames for one of a character's garments that action is listed under `missing` in custom.json instead of being drawn half-dressed (a leather/robe belt is simply left out of the kneel). The wooden sword is the generator's arming sword (copper variant) with the copper colours swapped for the generator's oak wood palette.

Derivations by the DurableMMO project: recoloring with generator palettes; walk+slash sheets stacked into one sheet; the eye layer is the eye-colored pixels extracted from the body's head sheet; shirt pixels that the LPC head covers (head zPos 100 > torso 35) were cleared so the layers can be drawn in the order given in custom.json. These derivatives keep the original licenses; where CC-BY-SA 3.0 or GPL is chosen they are shared under the same license.

Most files are multi-licensed: you may use any ONE of the licenses listed for that file (e.g. OGA-BY 3.0 or CC-BY-SA 3.0 or GPL 3.0). All listed authors must be credited.
